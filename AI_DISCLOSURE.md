# AI use disclosure

This project was developed with the help of AI coding tools, which were used for implementation, debugging, refactoring, testing, and documentation.

The maintainer reviews and tests all AI-assisted changes before they are merged. Raw prompts, chat transcripts, temporary agent worktrees, and other editor or runtime state are deliberately left out of the repository, since they are byproducts of development rather than part of the source.

Bug reports and contributions are judged on their technical merit, whether or not AI tools were involved.
